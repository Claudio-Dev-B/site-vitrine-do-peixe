The problem

Great producers only sell to people they already know.

There are Amazonian producers with quality fish and no buyer outside their own region. And there are buyers looking for a reliable supplier with no idea where to start.

Vitrine was built to close that gap. It is part of the Bússola do Peixe Amazônico project, a startup born in the Amazon: a place where producers get seen and buyers find them.

FAQ

What everyone asks before getting started.

How much does Vitrine cost?

Nothing. Sign-up and access are free for producers and buyers.

Does Vitrine charge commission on sales?

No. Bússola does not step into the negotiation or charge commission. Price, volume and logistics are agreed between you and the other party.

How do I know the producers are reliable?

Every profile is verified by our team before it appears on the platform. After the first contact, the conversation and the deal are between you.

What happens after I sign up?

You create your account and choose your profile. Every sign-up is approved by our team before first access to the platform.

Who can take part?

Producers and fish farmers, buyers and industries working with Amazonian fish.

Do I have to negotiate through Vitrine?

No. Vitrine connects both ends and the negotiation happens directly between you, in whatever way works best for both.
